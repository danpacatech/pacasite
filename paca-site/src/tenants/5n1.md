Iron Phoenix Martial Arts is a Tae Kwon Do dojo on the 5th floor of the PACA building, built around discipline, respect, confidence, and determination.

Classes meet Tuesdays and Thursdays: 5:30 to 6:30 pm. Cost is $50 a month for up to four family members.

About the master instructor: Master Len Kabasinski is a 5th degree Black Belt in Tae Kwon Do and a native of Erie, PA. His goal is to create a safe and affordable environment for people of all ages and abilities who want to train in martial arts, and he has experience teaching individuals with Autism and ADHD. Len is a nationally certified Health and Wellness coach who believes martial arts should positively affect both body and mind, with a dojo grounded in physical fitness, respect, focus, discipline, determination, confidence, and positive personal development.
