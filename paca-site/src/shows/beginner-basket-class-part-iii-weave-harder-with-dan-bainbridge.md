**Beginner Basket Class Part III: Weave Harder, with Dan Bainbridge**

Tuesday, November 17 | 6:00 to 8:00 (ish) PM

PACA Building, 1505 State St., Erie, PA 16501

$20 per person. 

First came the original, then came The Sequel. Now Dan Bainbridge of DBB Basketry returns for the third installment. You’ll weave your own pen and pencil holder basket using standard techniques and materials. No experience is needed and all supplies are included, so you don’t have to have seen the first two to enjoy this one.

Space is limited, so grab your spot early. Scan the QR code or visit paca1505.org for tickets.
