**Advanced Basket Course with Dan Bainbridge (4 weeks)**
Tuesdays, December 1, 8, 15 & 22 | 6:00 to 8:00 PM
PACA Building, 1505 State St., Erie, PA 16501
$75 per person for the full 4-week course

You’ve seen the original. You’ve seen The Sequel. You’ve seen Weave Harder. Now basket weaving at PACA is a 4-part series. Dan Bainbridge of DBB Basketry is back with an advanced course for weavers ready to take the next step. Over four Tuesday evenings in December you’ll learn advanced weaving techniques and finish two complete baskets to take home. All materials are included.This is a full course, not a drop-in class. One $75 registration covers all four sessions, and it’s priced and planned as a complete series. Taking the beginner class first is recommended.
