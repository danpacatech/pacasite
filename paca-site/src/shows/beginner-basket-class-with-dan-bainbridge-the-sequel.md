Ever wanted to try your hand at basket weaving? Here's your chance!

Join local basketry artist Dan Bainbridge at PACA for a beginner-friendly class where you'll weave your very own pen and pencil holder basket using traditional techniques and materials. Whether you're a lifelong crafter or picking up something new for the first time, this laid-back evening is a great way to make something with your hands (and maybe a new friend or two). $20 includes instruction and materials.
