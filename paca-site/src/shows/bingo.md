Cash prizes, basket raffles, and a room full of people who take this very seriously.

Join the Performing Arts Collective Alliance for an afternoon of Cash Bingo at the JPT Foundation Event Center.

**WHEN:** Sunday, September 20, 2026Doors open at 1:00 PM Bingo starts at 2:00 PM

**WHERE:** JPT Foundation Event Center2319 West 38th Street, Erie, PA 16506

**TICKETS:** $30 per ticket at paca1505.org

**ALSO HAPPENING:** 50/50 drawings Basket raffles, Additional raffles, BYOB and snacks (bring your own, and bring enough to share if you are feeling generous)

Bring your own dauber if you have a lucky one. We will not judge your superstitions. We work in theatre.

Every ticket supports PACA, Erie's black box theatre and arts collective at 1505 State Street. Your seat at the bingo table pays for the lights, the sound, and the seasons of live theatre that happen under them.
