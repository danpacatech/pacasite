Pour the eggnog and adjust the rabbit ears.

Have Yourself a Hammy Little Christmas is a live comedy spectacular at PACA, running Friday, December 11 and Saturday, December 12 at 7:30 PM. Staring Lisa Ann The Glam Ham and friends. Picture the holiday variety hour that used to come out of a wood-paneled television set every December, then put it on a black box stage in downtown Erie... a string of lights and more tinsel than anyone asked for.


Two nights only, at 1505 State Street.
