**Uncle Vanya**
*by Anton Chekhov, translated by Heidi Schreck*
Directed by Shawn Clerkin

Sonia and her uncle, Vanya, have devoted their lives to managing the family farm in isolation. When her celebrated, ailing father and his charismatic wife move in, their lives are upended. In the heat of the summer, the wrong people fall in love, desires and resentments erupt, and the family is forced to reckon with the ghosts of their unlived lives. This strikingly immediate translation pairs Chekhov's enduring masterpiece with one of America's most celebrated contemporary playwrights.

Heidi Schreck's adaptation of Chekhov's *Uncle Vanya* premiered on Broadway at Lincoln Center Theater's Vivian Beaumont Theater on April 24, 2024, directed by Lila Neugebauer.
