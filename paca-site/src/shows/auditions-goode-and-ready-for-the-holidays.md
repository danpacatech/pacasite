We are in need of comedic actors and singers for an evening of short plays, written by Jeff Goode, along with seasonal songs to make for a unique and hilarious production,filled with holiday spirit! Performance dates are Friday & Saturday, December 4 & 5, along with a matinee at 2 o’clock on Sunday, December 6. 

When: Monday, September 14th, 6 PM

Where: PACA - 1505 State Street, 2nd Floor

What to bring:  Yourself.  If you would like to sing, be prepared to sing a holiday song ( you may bring a recording or accompanist, if your would like).  For the one-acts.we will be doing cold reading from the script.  

Perusal scripts are available. Reach out to Jeff Rodland, if you would like a peruas script, or cannot make it to the auditions, but are interested in the show.
