When intoxication feels like the only way to survive the modern world, how can Emma ever sober up? A vibrant play that highlights the connections between socio-political context and addiction through a theatrical lens. People, Places & Things by Duncan Macmillin is one of the most intense shows ever presented on an Erie stage.  Directed by JR Fabin, assisted by Marnie Foss. Auditions are scheduled for Sun/Mon November 29th and 30th (or by special arrangement if you can't attend those dates). Show dates are March 12-27.
