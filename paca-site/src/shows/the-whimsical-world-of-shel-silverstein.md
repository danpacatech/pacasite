PACA presents an evening built entirely from the imagination of Shel Silverstein: funny, tender, a little weird, and just like life. Expect the wit, wordplay, and warmth that made Silverstein a household name, brought to the stage through poems, songs, and stories for anyone who never quite grew out of them. Directed by Teresa Testrake.

* Saturday, August 22 at 7:30 PM
* Sunday, August 23 at 2:00 PM
