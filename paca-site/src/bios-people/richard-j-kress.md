bio: >-
  Richard appears daily in the lifelong roles of husband to his wife Holly and
  father to his three exceptional children. The entire Kress family can be found
  on stages and behind the scenes all around the region. They reside in
  Waterford, PA and Richard works in Erie at Kress Financial Services.

  Richard has performed at PACA in Closer Than Ever and last year's Holiday Extravaganza as a soloist and with all 5 "Von Kress family singers" which was a very special experience. At the Station Dinner Theatre: John/Suzanne, NED Sullivan, and Steve Sullivan (in the Erie premier of Gnowarre). He often travels to Academy in Meadville and has portrayed: Sam Carmichael, Mister Sowerberry, Captain Von Trapp, Inspector Kemp, Victor Von Frankenstein, & Eugene Beauregarde, Agustín Magaldi. Favorite Erie Playhouse roles include Prince Topher and Bob Wallace. At Barrow: Topher (again). At Gannon: Herbie. Richard also sails and sings aboard the Victorian Princess for Italian dinner cruises with smooth crooner standards.

  Richard has a growing list of technical credits for sound, light, projection, and effects at Footlights, Academy, Barrow, Station Dinner Theatre, PACA, Penny & Pearl Productions, Behrend, and Gannon. 

  He also directs student productions at Robison Elementary.
