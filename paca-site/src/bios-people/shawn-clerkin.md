bio: Shawn Clerkin is a retired theatre professor and civic theatre artist who
  has been fortunate to have had opportunities to share his theatre talents with
  those of countless others in northwest Pennsylvania. He has performed and/or
  directed with the Academy Theatre (Meadville), the Barrow-Civic
  Theatre/Franklin Civic Operetta Association, Dafmark Dance Theatre, Dramashop,
  Erie Opera Theatre, the Erie Philharmonic, the Erie Playhouse, the
  Footlighters of Elk County, Gannon University's Schuster Theatre, Mercyhurst
  University's Institute for Arts and Culture, and the Roadhouse Theatre. This
  is his PACA debut, and he is grateful to have this opportunity to create with
  old and new collaborators in UNCLE VANYA's cast and crew.
