  Vanya*. He’s pretty sure The Neighbor Boy has one of the best jobs in theater:
  show up, say a few things, eat some snacks, and leave to read *Diary of a
  Wimpy Kid* backstage. London is 11 years old and a 5th grader at Rolling Ridge
  Elementary. He has been performing since he was 6 years old, and *Uncle
  Vanya* is his 24th show- which seems like a lot of shows for someone who still
  has homework. Some of London’s favorite roles have included Tiny Tim in *A
  Christmas Carol*, Sky the Girl Scout in *Beetlejuice Jr.*, Chip in *Beauty and
  the Beast*, Peter in *Finding Neverland*, and Mike Teavee in *Charlie and the
  Chocolate Factory*. He has loved his time at PACA over the years and is always
  happy for another chance to perform there. London would like to give a big
  thank-you to director Shawn Clerkin for including him in this production, and
  to the kind cast for welcoming him into the *Uncle Vanya* family. He hopes you
  enjoy the show- and don’t blink, or you might miss him!"
