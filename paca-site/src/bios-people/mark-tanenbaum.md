bio: >
  Before there was a PACA, there was a kid from Erie who fell in love with the
  stage.


  Mark Tanenbaum has been part of Erie’s arts scene since he first laced up his dance shoes with the Erie Civic Ballet Company. From there, he danced with the Chautauqua Dance Company, performed on every major stage in the market, and logged over 1,000 professional productions — including a long run with the comedy troupe In All Seriousness.


  In 2010, he turned a historic 1899 building on State Street into something Erie had never seen: a true home for independent, progressive performing arts. PACA was born.


  Since then, PACA has become more than a theater. It’s a gallery. A comedy club. A dance studio. A community. A place where Erie’s artists — from first-time performers to MFA graduates — have a stage to call their own.


  In 2020, Preservation Erie recognized PACA with a Preservation Excellence Award for breathing new life into downtown. And in 2025, the curtain rose on PACA’s 100th production.


  Mark is proof that one person’s vision — backed by grit, a deep love for this city, and more than a few curtain calls — can change a community.
