bio: >-
  Char is thrilled, as always, to be performing on the stage at PACA.  The first
  show she did at PACA was Night of the Living Dead (zombie ensemble) in 2019
  and she has been in many shows there since  - most recently in Bad Auditions
  by Bad Actors in July 2026.  In 2025, she had her first directing experience
  with Radium Girls on the PACA stage, and she is forever grateful to this Erie
  institution for the opportunites it has provided her.

  She is fortunate enough to have done shows at All an Act, Dramashop, the Erie Playhouse, the Station Dinner Theater, and Gannon University's Schuster Theater. In addition to stage work, she has also worked with Britty Lee and her companies Dreamlords Entertainment and Livealore Events, and was most recently seen as Wisteria, the Fairy Queen at the Gem City Renaissance Festival.  

  When not onstage, she is the owner of Creature Connection, LLC, a dog-walking and petsitting business. Her life pretty much consists of cats, dogs, and theater, which is a pretty awesome life.
