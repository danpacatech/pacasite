bio: >-
  Jade Mitchell has participated in the performing arts since age three,
  beginning as a classically trained dancer before transitioning to academic and
  community theatre. She earned degrees in Theatre from Gannon University and
  Design from Mercyhurst University.


  Jade was last seen on PACA’s stage as Davia in *Brownstone*. Her last Chekhov experience was performing as Dunyasha in *The Cherry Orchard* in 2012 on the Schuster Theatre stage.


  Jade believes deeply in the power of theatre, love, and community to transform lives both on and off the stage. She is also the founder and organizer of **Corgi Fest**, Erie’s most unique and whimsical annual festival.


  Jade is thrilled to return to the stage and to work once again with her beloved professor and mentor, Fr. Shawn Clerkin.
